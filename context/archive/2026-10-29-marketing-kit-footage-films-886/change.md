## Intent

Close [issue #886](https://github.com/SOFTURE/AI/issues/886): the kit renders films only from Playwright screen
recordings. Add a second film kind, `footage`, built from filmed clips (a person talking to camera plus b-roll):
probe and normalize the clips (rotation, HDR to SDR, mixed orientations), transcribe them with word times, cut
filler words and long pauses into an editable edit decision list, caption the cut, add transitions and motion,
lay ducked music under the voice, and render with the existing hyperframes pipeline. Every step is a standalone
library block; `renderFootageFilm(film, options)` runs the whole film without a `marketing.json`, and the CLI gets
`softure-marketing footage <video> [--step=transcribe|cut|render] [--commit] [--placeholder]`.

## Context

Labels: `enhancement`, `pkg: marketing-kit`. No version bump and no release in this change: one batch release
closes the wave (coordinator's brief). Paid providers (ElevenLabs Scribe, Voice Isolator, Music, Video to Music)
stay behind `--commit`; tests and CI use fake providers and `--placeholder`.

## Constraints

- The screen film stays as it is: no change to its config, composition or render.
- Nothing is paid for without `--commit`; a dry run prints what each paid step would bill.
- Fixture clips are CC0 and small: generated by ffmpeg at test time, nothing binary in the repository.
- Unit tests need no ffmpeg (the `test` CI job has none); the ffmpeg and render checks run in the opt-in render job.
