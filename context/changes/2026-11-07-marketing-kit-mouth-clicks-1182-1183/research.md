# Research

- `tools/marketing-kit/src/footage/declick.ts` (0.18.0, #1170): `findImpulses` (2-8 kHz, 2 ms frames, 1 ms hop,
  ±100 ms median, bursts under 12 ms, level's `jumpDb`, only inside `getRepairZones`), `repairSpan` (Burg AR order 24,
  least-squares interpolation, raised-cosine crossfade), `declickSamples` / `declickSpeech`, `describeDeclick`.
  The biquad band, the frame energies and `getRunningMedian` are private helpers there; a new detector needs them
  with a 10 kHz upper edge, so they move to a shared module (no behaviour change).
- The speech track the stage reads is cut from the clips' pre-gained sound (`prepareClipSounds`, -23 LUFS, #1159 /
  #1181), so the -45 dBFS rule applies to the stage's input as it is; `findMouthClicks` documents that its input is
  the pre-gained sound.
- Callers: `pipeline.ts` and `draft.ts` pass the level and log `describeDeclick`; `schema.ts` builds the enum from
  `DECLICK_LEVELS` (adding `repair` extends the config and `schema/marketing.schema.json`, regenerated by
  `npm run schema`).
- Word bounds on the real sound: a window that ends at the walked point (start) or begins at it (end) measures the
  sound on the far side only, so a sharp onset gives its own time instead of being smeared by half a window.
