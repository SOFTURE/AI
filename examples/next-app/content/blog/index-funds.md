An index fund buys every company in a market index instead of picking a few. Its expense ratio stays low because nobody is paid to choose.

## What you buy

One share of the fund holds a small slice of every company in the index.

## What it costs

The fund charges a yearly fee, taken from the money in it.[^fee]

## When it fits

It fits a saver who wants the market's return and can wait out its falls.

[^fee]: The fee is listed on the fund's factsheet.
